Skin Microneedling & Exosomes

Targeted treatments for texture, firmness and radiance.

Skin renewal

GENOSYS Microneedling with Power Solutions

A personalised treatment to help improve skin firmness, soften fine lines and wrinkles, and address uneven texture and certain types of pigmentation. I select a GENOSYS Power Solution to suit your skin, with hydrating options available, and finish each session with Dermalux LED light therapy.

What is microneedling?

Microneedling uses fine, sterile needles to create tiny, controlled channels in the skin. This stimulates natural collagen production to help improve skin firmness, smooth uneven texture and soften the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles.

Who are these treatments suitable for?

Microneedling may also help make enlarged pores, certain acne scars and some types of pigmentation less noticeable. It can improve the appearance of stretch marks on suitable areas of the body.

Treatment cannot go ahead if there is an active skin infection, a cold sore, inflamed acne or a flare-up of eczema or dermatitis in the area to be treated. A history of keloid scarring or poor wound healing may also make microneedling unsuitable.

What happens during treatment?

I cleanse and prepare your skin, using a gentle exfoliating peel where suitable. I then use the professional GENOSYS Needle Pen-K, which has adjustable needle settings from 0.25 to 2.0 mm. I tailor the setting to each area and the concern being treated.

GENOSYS Power Solutions

  • AWS — fine lines, wrinkles and loss of firmness
  • HES — hydration for dehydrated skin
  • CTS — uneven texture and the appearance of acne scars
  • SWS — uneven skin tone and certain types of pigmentation
  • CVS — soothing, hydration and support for the skin barrier
  • PCS — excess oil and congested pores

I select a formula to suit your skin. Where appropriate, I may combine compatible formulas to address more than one concern in the same session.

I carry out facial microneedling without numbing cream, and the treatment is generally well tolerated. You may feel a light prickling sensation in more sensitive areas. I finish each session with Dermalux LED light therapy.

When will I see results, and how many sessions will I need?

Results develop gradually over the following weeks. If hydration is your main goal, I may suggest starting with 3 sessions. For loss of firmness, I recommend a course of 6 sessions. For pigmentation or acne scarring, I advise on the number of sessions after assessing your skin. Sessions are spaced 4 weeks apart.

What are the possible side effects and downtime?

Redness usually settles within 24 hours. Your skin may temporarily feel warm, tight or sensitive. Mild swelling and tiny red dots at the needle sites can occur, and you may notice light flaking over the next few days.

Less common risks include infection and changes in pigmentation. Please contact me if redness, swelling or discomfort worsens.

How should I prepare and what aftercare should I follow?

Before treatment

Pause retinol, high-strength vitamin C and exfoliating acids 5–7 days before your appointment. Avoid tanning and let me know if the area to be treated is sunburnt or irritated.

After treatment

For the first 3 days, I recommend using GENOSYS Soothing Repair Post Cream to soothe and moisturise your skin.

For 72 hours, avoid make-up, strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and swimming. Avoid retinol, high-strength vitamin C, exfoliating acids and scrubs for the same period. Wait until your skin has settled before restarting them. Avoid direct sun and follow my advice on when to resume using broad-spectrum SPF.

Skin renewal

EXOSOME

Microneedling paired with a topical exosome serum for dull-looking skin, uneven texture and fine lines. Each session is tailored to your skin concerns. Available for the face and neck, with the option to include the décolletage.

What are exosomes and why are they used with microneedling?

Exosomes are tiny particles that carry signals between cells. In skincare, they’re being studied for their potential to influence how the skin responds to irritation and recovers after microneedling. One area of research is the skin’s protective barrier, which helps retain moisture and reduce feelings of dryness and tightness.

What’s in the serum?

I currently use SIMILDIET eXos Antiaging, a topical serum with plant-derived exosomes from lemon balm. It also contains hyaluronic acid to help retain moisture, along with seven biomimetic peptides, DMAE, hydrolysed DNA and RNA, minerals and mannan.

What can it help with?

Microneedling can help refine uneven texture and soften the appearance of fine lines. I tailor the treatment to your skin, including concerns such as dullness and dehydration. Changes develop gradually, and results vary from person to person.

Who is it suitable for?

I assess your skin before treatment. Please let me know if you have rosacea, very sensitive skin or lasting sensitivity after using a steroid cream on your face. I’ll postpone treatment if the area is actively inflamed, infected, sunburnt or has broken skin.

How many sessions will I need?

A course of four is available. I’ll recommend the number and spacing of sessions based on your skin concerns and how your skin responds.

What should I do before treatment?

Avoid retinol and exfoliating acids for at least three days before your appointment. If your skin is irritated or sunburnt, please contact me before attending.

What should I do after treatment?

Temporary redness, tightness, tiny pinpoint marks or light flaking can occur. Keep your skincare gentle and avoid retinol and exfoliating acids for at least 72 hours. Avoid make-up, strenuous exercise, saunas and swimming while your skin settles. I’ll explain how to protect the treated skin from the sun and when to resume your usual routine.

02 / 06✳

HairGen Scalp Therapy

Microneedling and LED for gradual hair thinning.

Hair & scalp

GENOSYS HairGen Scalp Microneedling with LED

A targeted scalp treatment for clients concerned about gradual hair thinning. The GENOSYS HairGen Booster combines superficial microneedling with red and blue LED light and HR³ Matrix Hair Solution α.

What is HairGen scalp microneedling?

The GENOSYS HairGen Booster combines superficial scalp microneedling with red and blue LED light. Fine needles create tiny channels in the surface of the scalp as the device applies HR³ Matrix Hair Solution α.

Who are these treatments suitable for?

HairGen may be suitable if you’ve noticed that your hair is gradually becoming finer or less dense. If you’re shedding significantly more hair than usual, or if the loss is sudden, patchy or unexplained, speak to your GP first to help identify the cause. I can then assess your scalp and discuss whether HairGen is suitable for you.

Treatment cannot go ahead on infected, inflamed, broken or sunburnt skin. Please let me know before booking if you have a metal allergy or a tendency to develop keloid scars.

What happens during treatment?

I prepare your scalp, using a gentle scalp peel where suitable. I then part your hair and treat the selected areas. I open a new, single-use stamp with 0.3 mm microneedles and a fresh ampoule for each session. You may feel light tapping or mild prickling.

What’s in the solution?

Key ingredients include copper tripeptide-1 and other peptides, panthenol (provitamin B5), niacinamide (vitamin B3), saw palmetto and broccoli extracts, and menthol.

When will I see results, and how many sessions will I need?

Visible changes in hair density take time, and results vary. I offer a course of 6 sessions and agree the interval with you after assessing your scalp. At the end of the course, I review your progress with you and discuss whether further sessions are appropriate.

What are the possible side effects and downtime?

You may notice temporary redness, mild tenderness or sensitivity. If redness spreads, pain increases, or you notice discharge or develop a fever, seek medical advice and let me know.

How should I prepare and what aftercare should I follow?

Before treatment

Please arrive with freshly washed, dry hair and a clean scalp. Avoid dry shampoo, scalp oils, gels, hairspray and other styling products before your appointment.

After treatment

Avoid rubbing or scratching the treated areas. I’ll explain when you can wash your hair, go swimming, use a sauna and restart scalp products, including topical minoxidil if you use it.

03 / 06✳

HydrO2 Facial

Deep cleansing, exfoliation and hydration.

Advanced facial

HydrO2 Facial

A personalised facial that combines deep cleansing, exfoliation and hydration to leave your skin feeling smoother and looking fresher.

What is the Hydro2 Facial?

Hydro2 Facial is a treatment using the 3D Aesthetics system to deep-cleanse, exfoliate and hydrate the skin.

  • Hydro Peel. HS1 uses glycolic and citric acids to lift away dead skin cells. HS2 contains 1% salicylic acid to help clear congested areas, while HS3 contains sodium hyaluronate and centella to hydrate the skin.
  • Capsule exfoliation. Depending on your skin’s needs, I use either the Anti-ageing/Brightening Formula or the Oxygenating and Hydrating formula.
  • Oxygenation. I use a dedicated handpiece to mist the skin with a hyaluronic acid solution.
  • Hydrating mask with ultrasound. I apply a hydrating mask and use the ultrasound handpiece over it.
  • Fire & Ice. A warming handpiece prepares the skin, and a cooling handpiece refreshes it towards the end of the treatment.

I finish with a serum and moisturiser chosen for your skin.

Hydro2 Facial Plus includes all the stages above, with the addition of radiofrequency (RF) to help improve the appearance of skin firmness and Electro-Ion to support the absorption of the products applied during treatment.

What can it help with?

This facial is a good option if you’re concerned about blocked pores, uneven texture, dehydration or dull-looking skin.

Who is it suitable for?

The facial is suitable for many dry, oily and combination skin types.

Please contact me before booking if you’re pregnant or have sunburn, broken skin, an active skin infection or a flare-up of eczema or dermatitis. Both capsule gels contain retinol.

Capsule exfoliation is unsuitable for very sensitive skin or active acne. A patch test is required before using a capsule formula if you’re prone to allergies or have previously experienced skin sensitivity.

What should I do before treatment?

Avoid retinol, exfoliating acids and facial scrubs for 3 days before your appointment. If you’ve recently had a peel, laser treatment or facial waxing, please check with me before booking.

What can I expect after treatment?

Your skin may look fresher and feel smoother after one session. Mild, temporary redness or sensitivity can occur. If irritation persists or worsens, please contact me.

What should I do after treatment?

  • For the first 24 hours, avoid make-up, swimming, saunas, steam rooms and strenuous exercise.
  • For 3 days, avoid retinol, exfoliating acids and scrubs. Use a gentle cleanser and moisturiser.
  • Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher daily. Avoid sunbeds and prolonged direct sun exposure while your skin settles.

How many treatments will I need?

You can book a single facial. For regular maintenance, every 4–6 weeks is a starting point.

04 / 06✳

HIFU Face & Neck

Focused ultrasound for a firmer-looking contour.

Firming & lifting

HIFU

A non-surgical lifting treatment that uses focused ultrasound to target deeper supporting tissues, including the SMAS where appropriate. HIFU helps firm the skin on the face and neck and define the jawline.

What is HIFU?

HIFU stands for high-intensity focused ultrasound. It uses focused heat beneath the skin to stimulate collagen renewal. Depending on the area treated, the ultrasound can reach the SMAS, a deeper layer that helps support the tissues of the face.

I use the 3D HIFU system from 3D Aesthetics (3D-lipo Ltd). I select two of the three cartridge depths (1.5 mm, 3.0 mm and 4.5 mm) for each area and use them in sequence. A full face and neck session takes around two hours.

What can it help with?

HIFU helps firm and tighten the skin on the face and neck. Depending on the area treated, the aim is a subtle lift around the brows or cheeks, or a more defined jawline.

Who is it suitable for?

HIFU is best suited to people with mild to moderate skin laxity.

I do not carry out HIFU if you have active cancer or are undergoing cancer treatment. If you’ve had cancer treatment in the past, please contact me before booking.

Please also contact me before booking if you’re pregnant, breastfeeding, have a pacemaker, or have an implant, broken skin or an active infection in the treatment area.

What should I do before treatment?

Let me know if you’ve recently had injections, a peel or an energy-based treatment in the same area so I can advise on timing.

What can I expect after treatment?

Temporary redness, swelling or tenderness can occur after the session. Bruising is uncommon.

Visible changes typically develop over 2–3 months and can continue to improve for up to 6 months.

What should I do after treatment?

For the first 24 hours, avoid saunas, steam rooms and hot baths. Use gentle skincare and apply sunscreen daily.

If you notice an unusual reaction or your symptoms worsen, contact me promptly. If symptoms are severe, seek medical advice.

How many treatments will I need?

HIFU is performed in one session. If a maintenance session is needed, it can be planned 9–12 months later.

05 / 06✳

Microcurrent Facial

Gentle toning and lymphatic drainage massage.

Facial sculpting

Microcurrent

A gentle facial that combines microcurrent toning for a more lifted look with lymphatic drainage massage to help reduce facial and under-eye puffiness.

What is Microcurrent?

Microcurrent uses gentle electrical pulses to stimulate the facial muscles. I use the Zemits Adrinox 2.0 with facial probes and a conductive gel suited to your skin, such as Zemits GloryTight. The 45-minute session includes lymphatic drainage massage with conductive gloves.

What can it help with?

Microcurrent temporarily tones the skin for a firmer, more lifted look. The massage helps reduce visible facial puffiness, including under-eye puffiness caused by fluid build-up.

Who is it suitable for?

Microcurrent is suited to clients who have noticed early signs of loss of facial firmness.

Microcurrent is unsuitable during pregnancy or if you have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device. Contact me before booking if you have epilepsy, a heart condition or a metal implant, or if you have broken skin or an active infection in the treatment area.

What should I do before treatment?

Please arrive without make-up if possible. Let me know if you’ve recently had injections, a peel or another facial treatment.

What can I expect after treatment?

You may notice a subtle toning effect after one session. There’s usually no downtime.

What should I do after treatment?

You can usually resume your normal skincare straight away. If your skin feels sensitive, use gentle products. Contact me if you notice an unusual reaction.

How many treatments will I need?

I recommend 10–12 sessions, starting twice a week and then moving to once a week.

Dermalux LED Therapy

Targeted light therapy for skin and recovery.

LED light therapy

Dermalux LED Therapy

Targeted LED light therapy for temporary relief of localised muscle and joint pain. Facial sessions help calm inflamed breakouts and visible redness and support skin recovery.

What is Dermalux LED Therapy?

Dermalux is a non-invasive light treatment for the face and body. I use the Dermalux Flex MD and select blue, red or near-infrared light, alone or in combination, to suit the area and your concerns. Facial sessions last 30 minutes, and protective eyewear is provided.

What can it help with?

  • Localised muscle and joint pain. Red and near-infrared light can temporarily ease pain and stiffness, such as a sore forearm after repetitive work or an aching shoulder or knee after physical activity.
  • Acne and skin recovery. Blue light helps reduce inflamed breakouts on the face, chest or back. Near-infrared light helps calm visible redness and supports skin recovery as spots settle or after treatments such as microneedling.
  • Fine lines and uneven texture. Red light helps soften the appearance of fine lines and improve the look of uneven texture.

Who is it suitable for?

Facial LED suits a wide range of skin types and can be combined with selected skin treatments.

LED therapy is unsuitable if you have light-triggered seizures, systemic lupus (SLE), active cancer or light sensitivity caused by a condition or medication. Please contact me before booking if you’re pregnant, have an eye condition or experience light-triggered migraines.

What should I do before treatment?

For facial LED, please arrive without make-up if possible. Let me know about any medication you take or recent procedures in the same area. If you have new or unexplained pain or swelling, seek medical advice before booking a body session.

What can I expect after treatment?

There’s usually no downtime. Mild warmth or redness can occur and typically settles within an hour or two. A temporary headache is rare.

What should I do after treatment?

You can continue with your usual skincare. If LED was part of another procedure, follow that procedure’s aftercare. Contact me if you notice an unusual reaction.
